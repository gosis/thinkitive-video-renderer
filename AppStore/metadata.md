## Why these words

- **"Handwritten Captions"** is in the name because no app in the top results has those words in its name, and the leaders for that search have around 5k ratings, so it's a niche Inkcap can own. The name is the strongest ranking signal.
- **The subtitle** carries the big generic searches ("animated text", "auto captions", "subtitles"), where apps with 100k–2.5M ratings dominate.
- **The keyword field** adds terms Apple combines with the name and subtitle: *add* + *video* match "add text to video", plus "handwriting video", "write on video", "cursive" and use cases. No word is repeated across fields.
- Competitor brand names (TikTok, Instagram…) must not be used in any field.
